Nice to meet you. I'm Emily, an aspiring UX designer based in South Florida. Currently pursuing my Bachelor's in Information Technology at Indian River State College, I combine technical expertise with a passion for eye-catching, purposeful design.

My background in customer service and technical support has given me a unique perspective on user needs — I've helped 2,000+ customers navigate complex challenges, shaping an empathetic approach to every design problem.

I'm particularly interested in building digital tools for the fiber arts community, bringing together my love for crochet, knitting, and thoughtful UX.
