About Me

Hi! I'm Emily, a UX designer based in Port Saint Lucie, Florida. Currently pursuing my Bachelor's in Information Technology at Indian River State College, I combine technical expertise with a passion for creating user-centered designs.

My background in customer service and technical support has given me a unique perspective on understanding user needs and pain points. I've helped over 300+ customers navigate complex technical challenges, which has shaped my empathetic approach to design.

I'm particularly interested in designing digital tools and experiences for the fiber arts community—bringing together my love for crochet, knitting, and thoughtful UX design.
